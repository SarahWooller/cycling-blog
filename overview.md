I want to create a website to act as a platform for blogs about cycling, using npm vite and next.js.

I'll start with a sample blog page.   
The photos will be hosted on my google photos in a shared album (eg the first 15 of  https://photos.app.goo.gl/Q4mtvxKg9pt9RoJZ9)
and the text will be cut and pasted or linked from a shared google doc. (eg https://docs.google.com/document/d/1e30mZiEnBOU7oP8rpqcnZXahBderJpMOMv60sJdxTRs/edit?usp=sharing)
The page should have the facility to add these links.

One photo (to be chosen by the user) will be used as the backdrop to blog - reduced to blue and white tones. 
The part that acts as a frame for the blog will be left in full vibrancy, 
the part behind the text will be substantially lighter so that there is good contrast with the words.

There will be a band to the right of the page where the other photos appear as one enlarged photo with thumb-prints of the others (max 15 total) 
beneath it.

There will also be a box beneath the thumb-prints allowing for a summary and the inclusion of several hyper-links.
Optionally there will be a last box at the bottom on the right for a permanent photo, normally of the map of the route.
